Our Story

A Family Tradition
Since 1998

La Bella Cucina was born from a simple dream: to bring the true flavors of the Italian countryside to New York City. Founded by the Rossetti family, our kitchen has been crafting handmade pastas, slow-simmered sauces, and wood-fired dishes with the same love and care since the day we opened.

Every ingredient is sourced with intention — local produce, imported Italian staples, and family recipes passed down through three generations. When you dine with us, you're not just eating — you're stepping into our home.
